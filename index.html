Account Deletion Request

Delete your account

You can request deletion of your Deluvia account and the personal data associated with it. Account deletion is permanent.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to the developer using the contact address below.
  2. Use the subject Deluvia Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Deluvia account.
  4. We may contact you to verify ownership of the account.

Important: Sending a request does not itself delete your account. We will verify and process valid requests and confirm when deletion is complete.

What will be deleted?

We will delete your Firebase Authentication account and associated personal data that Deluvia controls, including applicable profile information and user-related records.

Some information may be retained when legally required or for legitimate security purposes. If any data must be retained, we will explain what is retained and why.
