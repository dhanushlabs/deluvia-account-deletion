Account management

Request account deletion

Your privacy matters to us. If you no longer wish to use Deluvia, you can request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your Deluvia account:

  1. Click the email button on this page.
  2. Send your request using the email address associated with your Deluvia account, if possible.
  3. Use the subject Deluvia Account Deletion Request.
  4. Complete any reasonable verification needed to confirm account ownership.

Please note: Sending a request does not immediately delete your account. We will verify and process valid requests and confirm when deletion is complete.

What data will be deleted?

When your request is processed, we will delete your Deluvia account and associated personal data that we control, including applicable account profile information and user-related records.

Certain information may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law or for legitimate security purposes. Any applicable retention will be explained to you.

What happens after I submit a request?

We will review your request, verify ownership where necessary, and process it as soon as reasonably possible. We may contact you if additional information is required.
